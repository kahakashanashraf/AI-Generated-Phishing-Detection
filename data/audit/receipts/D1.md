### Interpretation and limitations
- The file is **single-label** as observed: no `label=0` controls; a self-contained phishing-vs-legitimate classifier **cannot** be trained, cross-validated, or tested here. It may supply *positive phishing emails* only after source-label and rights audit.
- Independent non-authoritative mirror reports `1=phishing, 0=legitimate` and independently lists 1,565 all-positive Nazario records: https://huggingface.co/datasets/kudzaiprichard/aura-phishing-email-corpus . This **supports but does not independently adjudicate** true maliciousness; source corpus documentation and manual quality review are still needed.
- **POTENTIAL LEAKAGE:** the mirror lists `Nazario_5.csv` with the *same 1,565 Nazario phishing messages* plus 1,500 purported legitimate emails; `Nazario.csv` and `Nazario_5.csv` **must never be treated as independent sources, splits or replications** without record-level de-duplication. The overlap claim has not yet been tested on our local data.
- **POTENTIAL NON-EMAIL/ADMIN RECORD:** external downstream corpus documentation flags an instruction/admin message in Nazario; inspect count privately and adjudicate before excluding any row. No raw content should be displayed or committed.
- **Rights:** the Zenodo HTML page's `Rights > License` text remains blank to our browser; the NLM dataset catalog and a related Figshare repository separately report **CC BY 4.0** for the curated dataset (https://datasetcatalog.nlm.nih.gov/dataset?q=0002120019 ; https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Phishing_Email_Curated_Datasets/24899943). This is **secondary support only**; verify the authoritative repository metadata and **upstream Nazario constituent corpus rights** independently before any redistribution. A collection-level CC license cannot automatically override constituent third-party rights.
- CSV emptiness heuristic is **not** a full data-quality assessment: duplicates, URL validity, campaign labels, date parsing, nuanced null literals, author/generator provenance, and privacy remain unverified.
