# Deep Related-Work Evidence Matrix — 30 selected studies

**Date:** 2026-10-24. **Scope:** phishing-vs-legitimate email, AI provenance and rewriting, counterfactual NLP, source shift, and deployment risk. **Not a PRISMA systematic review.** These are the 30 exactly used in [related_work.tex](related_work.tex) and [references.bib](references.bib).

## Evidence integrity
A full-text or Related Work read is claimed **only** when the status explicitly says so. Abstract-only entries are substantive but limited for methods; do not invent train/test details or absence of experiments. URL-only phishing research was excluded from these 30 because the primary task is *email*. Preprints are marked. Detailed comparisons are based on the stated task, methods and documented evidence, not incompatible headline accuracy.

## Major synthesis findings

1. **The task-label trap:** human-written phishing vs machine phishing is attribution, **not** phishing-vs-legitimate classification. Do not combine numerical results or relabel dataset outcomes (Gutierrez, CuePhish, MAGE).
2. **The novelty trap:** the CheckList invariance and directional-expectation framework, Gardner contrast sets and Kaushik label-flipping counterfactuals predate this project. The proposed two-sided loss is **not** a newly invented concept. A paper needs audited *security-specific evidence* plus a demonstrable controlled benefit.
3. **The robustness trap:** rewriting and augmentation (Ferrell, Nance, Kulal), modern synthetic datasets (E-PhishGEN), variant flip scores (PhishFuzzer), cross-corpus/prevalence (Bhuiyan), GenAI semantic stability/gating (Arharbi) are all already researched. Incremental architecture fusion without comparisons would be weak.
4. **The ground-truth trap:** changes to sender, URL or payment request are not automatically label flips without sender trust, domain ownership and task context. Ambiguous edits must be excluded, not labeled by an LLM alone.
5. **The testing trap:** exact/near duplicates, campaign siblings, LLM generator labels, source versions and target-threshold tuning can invalidate leave-one-LLM-out evidence.
6. **The deployment trap:** PR-AUC alone is insufficient; record actual false alarm, phishing recall and review workload at fixed development-only thresholds. Risk coverage/shift bounds must not be overstated.

## Pre-registered research claims permitted at this stage
A *research question* is permitted; a *validated result* is not. Cite these 30 accurately; do not claim 'first bidirectional consistency', 'first LLM rewrite robustness', or 'first domain shift evaluation'. Candidate gain: dataset-parent/source-aware, human-validated security-equivalent and security-changing pairs under frozen operating constraints. **M0 remains conditional NO-GO for a strong novelty assertion.**
