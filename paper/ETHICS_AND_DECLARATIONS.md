## Ethics Statement (for the manuscript and the submission form)

> This study did not recruit or involve human participants. All analyses use publicly released email corpora and text generated by openly licensed language models. The only human judgements are those of two of the authors, who rated a sample of generated emails as part of the study's validation (intent preservation, label preservation and plausibility). Because the raters are the investigators rating text, not participants providing data about themselves, the study does not constitute human subjects research, and no ethics committee approval was sought. The work was carried out at EliteLab, an independent research group without an institutional review board; it was not conducted under the supervision of, or with resources from, any university.
>
> The email corpora contain messages written by real individuals. In the authors' assessment, the study poses no additional risk to these individuals or to their privacy, for five reasons:
> (i) every corpus was already publicly released by its custodians, and each was used under its stated licence or terms;
> (ii) no attempt was made to identify, contact or profile any individual;
> (iii) no email text is redistributed: the released materials contain only pseudonymous sample identifiers that can be derived from the public sources, scores, decisions and aggregate statistics;
> (iv) generated phishing text is never released, and any examples quoted in the paper are defanged and stripped of personal details;
> (v) no new phishing content was written from scratch: phishing rewrites were produced only from already-public phishing emails and keep their original links.
